## Exercise photos

The start and end photos under `public/exercise-photos/` come from
**yuhonas/free-exercise-db**, https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db,
revision `f00c92c7dcf1216a928a52c3706c7ce8e2f71ed5`, which its repository
releases into the public domain under the Unlicense
(https://unlicense.org). They were resized and converted to WebP.

That repository restructures an earlier dataset, `wrkout/exercises.json`. The
original source of the photographs was not traced beyond it, so the
public-domain dedication is relied on as stated and has not been verified
further. `docs/CATALOG.md` describes how to remove every photo.
